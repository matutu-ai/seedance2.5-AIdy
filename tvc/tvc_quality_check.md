# TVC Quality Check

Before delivery, check:

- Character: same face, clothing, age, body proportion, and motion causality.
- Product: same shape, material, color, logo boundary, and functional behavior.
- Scene: no spatial drift, impossible geography, or unmotivated lighting change.
- Commercial: clear hook, pain, value, brand memory, and audience relevance.
- Camera: every shot has position, lens, movement, focus, and depth.
- Seedance: duration, 16:9, timeline, sound, negative prompt, and end state are present.
- AI risk: no extra limbs, broken hands, duplicate objects, unreadable text, physics errors, or abrupt identity changes.

## Director output gate

The final package must contain Brand Insight, Big Idea, Creative Concept, TVC Script, Scene Breakdown, Storyboard, Camera Language, Seedance Prompt, Sound Design, and Post Production.

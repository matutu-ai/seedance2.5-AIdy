# TVC Camera Language

Useful commercial choices:

| Tool | Use |
|---|---|
| Macro shot | material, texture, precision |
| Hero shot | product authority and final recall |
| Dolly in | discovery, pressure, confidence |
| Tracking shot | user/product journey |
| Crane shot | scale and reveal when space supports it |
| Slow motion | tactile emphasis; keep physics credible |
| Product rotation | controlled form and material read |
| Extreme close-up | decisive detail or emotion |

Camera baseline: ARRI Alexa 35 or equivalent cinematic live-action capture, optional anamorphic lens, shallow depth of field only when focus remains readable. Do not stack incompatible movements in one shot.

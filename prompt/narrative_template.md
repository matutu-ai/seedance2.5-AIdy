# Narrative Template

Use for a character turn. Design a single shot or divide an extended story into independently generatable beats.

```markdown
Premise and dramatic question: [who wants what, and what threatens it]
Continuity anchors: [identity, wardrobe, prop, space, time of day]
Beat 1 [time]: [baseline action and composition]
Beat 2 [time]: [trigger and performance change]
Beat 3 [time]: [peak choice/reaction and camera response]
Beat 4 [time]: [resolution/end frame]
Performance: [face, eye line, breath, posture, hands]
Camera/light/world/realism: [director-grade specific instructions]
Negative constraints and parameters: [target-model-safe settings]
```

# Acting Library

Use one primary state plus a transition; do not paste every cue at once.

| State | Inner condition | Face and eyes | Body | Prompt expression |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Smile | warmth or social mask | eye corners soften; restrained lip lift | shoulders release | “a small smile reaches her eyes, then fades” |
| Crying | grief breaking through | wet lower lids; jaw tremor; gaze drops | breath catches; chest contracts | “she exhales unevenly before one tear escapes” |
| Anger | controlled threat | fixed gaze; brows lower; jaw sets | still torso; fist tightens | “his jaw locks while his hand slowly closes” |
| Fear | danger appraisal | pupils search; lips part | shoulders rise; weight retreats | “her eyes scan first, then her body pulls back” |
| Surprise | sudden new information | blink pause; eyes widen | breath stops; head draws back | “a half-beat of stillness precedes the recoil” |
| Hesitation | competing choices | gaze alternates; lips press | hand hovers; weight shifts | “her hand stops just short of the handle” |
| Resolve | decision after doubt | focus steadies; brow releases | spine lengthens; step commits | “she steadies her breath and moves forward” |

# Cinematic Terms

- **Blocking:** planned placement and movement of performers in space.
- **Screen direction:** consistent perceived left/right movement across cuts.
- **Eyeline:** where a performer looks; it must agree with object and reverse-shot placement.
- **Coverage:** complementary views available to tell a scene; a generated clip normally needs one primary view.
- **Beat:** a small unit of action or emotional change.
- **Rack focus:** deliberate focus shift between depth planes.
- **Parallax:** relative movement of foreground and background during camera travel.
- **Negative space:** intentionally empty frame area that conveys isolation or anticipation.
- **Practical effect:** real-world source/action in the scene, such as rain, wind, a lamp, or smoke.
- **Continuity:** stable identity, wardrobe, props, lighting logic, direction, and spatial relationships across a shot/sequence.

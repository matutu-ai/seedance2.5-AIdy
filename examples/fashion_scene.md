# Fashion Scene — Windy Rooftop Editorial

## Copy-ready prompt

# Cinematic Scene

## Story Context
Blue-hour rooftop above a dense city. A model in a sculptural ivory coat pauses at the parapet, turning toward camera with controlled confidence.

## Character Acting
Her gaze begins on the skyline, then meets the lens without smiling. A slow inhale lifts her collarbone; one hand gathers the coat lapel, releases it, and her chin settles slightly lower in quiet resolve.

## Emotion Timeline
0–3: poised stillness. 3–6: wind catches the coat and she controls the fabric. 6–8: direct eye contact. 8–10: a measured quarter-turn ends in profile.

## Body Movement
She stays one meter from the parapet, feet planted; her coat moves with the wind but never changes design or length.

## Camera Direction
A 50 mm camera at chest height begins medium-wide, makes a smooth rightward 60-degree arc to medium close-up, then holds. Gentle focus transition from the moving lapel to her eyes; stabilized movement with no handheld shake.

## Lighting
Soft cool sky key from screen left, warm city windows as distant practical bokeh, thin rim along the coat's right edge. Highlights glide across the fabric as she turns.

## Environment
Glass towers, damp concrete, distant traffic; consistent wind moves hair, coat hem, and a loose ribbon in the same direction.

## Background Behavior
Tiny traffic streams below; one aircraft light crosses far background.

## Physical Realism
Fine fabric responds to gusts and gravity, realistic hair strands, shallow depth of field, controlled motion blur in the coat hem.

## Negative Prompt
No altered garment design, extra limbs, warped face, wind from conflicting directions, plastic fabric, text, watermark, logo.

## Model Parameters
Seedance 2.5, 10 seconds, 9:16, high-end live-action fashion editorial.

# 《当AI成为运营伙伴》

## Theme

GEO运营人的AI成长之旅：AI changes how companies operate.

## Character bible

`CHARACTER_GEO_OPERATOR`: same face, age impression, hairstyle, body proportions, dark practical wardrobe, and persistent notebook across all shots. Motion progresses from tense scanning to focused learning to confident presentation.

## Venue lock

The office and training hall are separate stable spaces. In the hall, the stage screen is far end, entrance is camera-left, aisle is camera-right, and audience faces the stage. Wide, left, right, stage, and audience references form one venue map.

## Three-act structure

```text
0-5s Hook: scattered GEO notes in a cold blue office.
5-15s Problem / Transformation: the operator enters the AI training hall and follows a clear method.
15-25s Solution / Value: the operator and team turn questions into a visible plan.
25-30s Memory point: warm light, confident presentation, readable brand end state.
```

## Camera and lighting

35mm slow dolly in and rack focus in the office; tracking through the fixed right aisle in the hall; final restrained arc toward the stage screen. Cold blue office light -> blue-purple stage light -> warm productivity light.

# Emotional Scene — Reunion

**Seedance 2.5 assumption:** 12 seconds, 16:9, naturalistic drama.

## Copy-ready prompt

# Cinematic Scene

## Story Context
At a quiet train platform at dawn, a man sees his sister after many years. His goal is to reach her, but disbelief holds him in place before the reunion.

## Character Acting
He recognizes her with a tiny pause: his eyes fix on her scarf, then rise to her face. His breath catches, lower eyelids wet, jaw loosens, and one hand hovers near his coat seam. He suppresses a smile before it breaks through; his shoulders release as he takes one step toward her.

## Emotion Timeline
0–3: guarded waiting. 3–6: recognition, eyes lock. 6–9: breath breaks and restrained tears form. 9–12: he exhales, smiles softly, and begins the approach.

## Body Movement
He stands two meters from camera near a platform column, facing screen right; his sister remains eight meters ahead, facing him. His single step preserves the distance and screen direction.

## Camera Direction
A stable 85 mm medium close-up at eye height starts on him, then makes a slow 20-centimeter dolly-in. Focus shifts briefly from the scarf in foreground to his eyes at second four; the camera holds through the final step.

## Lighting
Cool blue dawn fills the platform, with a warm train-window practical behind his sister. Soft key from the open sky, no harsh fill; as he turns, the warm practical creates a subtle rim on his cheek.

## Environment
Condensed breath, quiet rail mist, damp concrete, stationary train.

## Background Behavior
A porter pushes a luggage cart slowly in deep background; a departure board flickers once. Neither interrupts the reunion.

## Physical Realism
Natural eye moisture, shallow focus falloff, slight lens breathing in the focus pull, realistic coat fabric shifting with the step.

## Negative Prompt
No exaggerated sobbing, frozen expression, doubled people, face changes, floating luggage, subtitles, text, watermark, or abrupt zoom.

## Model Parameters
Seedance 2.5, 12 seconds, 16:9, realistic live-action drama.

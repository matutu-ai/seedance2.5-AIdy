# Cinematic Scene

## Story Context
A sleek unbranded electric sedan moves silently through a rain-clean city at blue hour, arriving at a glass-fronted cultural center. The dramatic turn is from fluid motion to a calm, confident hero stop.

## Character Acting
The driver is an Asian woman around 30, with short black hair, a tailored charcoal blazer, and an ivory shirt. Her face is partially shaped by passing city reflections rather than presented as an identifiable portrait. She holds the wheel with both hands, shoulders relaxed and back supported by the seat. Her eyes stay focused on the road, briefly checking the right mirror before returning forward; her lips remain naturally closed and her breathing is calm. Before the entrance turn, her right fingers make one precise steering correction. After the car stops, she exhales softly and her shoulders release by a fraction, conveying quiet control.

## Emotion Timeline
0–3: composed forward momentum. 3–6: the vehicle glides past reflected city lights. 6–9: camera reveals the sculpted side and wheels. 9–12: it stops cleanly in a three-quarter hero composition.

## Body Movement
The sedan travels from screen left to right on the near lane, then turns gently toward the entrance. The driver remains visible from chest-up in side and three-quarter views; her head and shoulders follow the turn with a tiny natural delay. During braking, her torso shifts forward by a few centimeters and settles back against the seat. Both hands keep real contact with the wheel; sleeves, collar, and hair respond subtly to breath and vehicle inertia. Tires remain planted; suspension compresses subtly at the final stop.

## Camera Direction
Low 35 mm tracking camera begins one meter ahead of the front-left corner, matches speed, then drifts alongside the driver-side profile. Between seconds four and six, it briefly peers through the rain-dotted side window to capture the driver's eyes, hands, and steering wheel. Focus shifts from her eyes to her hands, then returns to the headlamp and front contour. At second eight it cranes slightly to a three-quarter front hero frame.

## Lighting
Cool blue ambient sky is the main light. Warm architectural practicals and streetlamps create controlled elongated reflections along the body; soft rear rim separates the silhouette. Reflections travel correctly as the camera and car move.

## Environment
Wet asphalt, modern glass facade, restrained city traffic, clean curb line, fine road spray behind wheels.

## Background Behavior
Pedestrians wait under the canopy; a cyclist passes in the opposite far lane. Building lights remain stable, with no distracting movement near the hero frame.

## Physical Realism
Wheel rotation and motion blur match speed; tire contact patches, subtle suspension movement, road spray, body reflections, and exposure adaptation are physically consistent.

## Negative Prompt
No distorted vehicle geometry, warped wheels, invented badges/logos, changing paint color, floating tires, face deformation, eye drift, robotic blinking, extra fingers, hands detached from the wheel, hand intersection, clothing flicker, body movement disconnected from vehicle inertia, unreadable interface text, excess traffic, watermark, text, or game-engine look.

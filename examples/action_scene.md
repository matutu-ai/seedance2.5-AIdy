# Action Scene — Abandoned Building Search

## Copy-ready prompt

# Cinematic Scene

## Story Context
Rainy night, abandoned municipal archive building. A young woman in a dark rain jacket enters with a compact pistol and red flashlight, searching for evidence of a missing person. A metallic impact from upstairs interrupts her controlled search.

## Character Acting
Her eyes search the doorways before her head follows. Shallow breaths lift her shoulders; her jaw tightens and the fingers around the pistol grip turn pale. At the impact, she freezes for half a beat, her lips part, then she steadies her breath and raises the flashlight toward the stairwell.

## Emotion Timeline
0–3 seconds: alert, contained entry. 3–6: the red beam finds the corridor directory. 6–9: distant impact causes a backward weight shift and widened eyes. 9–12: fear compresses into resolve as she takes one deliberate step forward.

## Body Movement
She enters along the corridor centerline, then stops beside the directory at frame left. Her boots splash shallow rainwater tracked inside; the pistol stays down until the sound. No abrupt teleporting or weapon morphing.

## Camera Direction
At shoulder height, a 40 mm camera starts two meters behind her in a medium rear three-quarter shot. It tracks forward along her left side into a close profile, slow and deliberate. At second six focus pulls from the red flashlight on the directory to her eyes; restrained handheld breathing settles after her recoil.

## Lighting
Cold blue streetlight spills through broken windows from screen right; flickering green fluorescent tubes give weak overhead fill. The flashlight paints a narrow red accent. As the camera reaches her profile, blue window light becomes a rim and her face falls into soft green half-shadow.

## Environment
Peeling paint, wet tile, a directory, stairwell door, drifting rain sound from the entrance. Dust and humidity remain subtle.

## Background Behavior
At the far end, a shadowed security guard shape crosses once behind frosted glass, never approaching. A loose report page skates along the wet floor when the draft arrives.

## Physical Realism
Natural shallow depth of field, directional motion blur on the moving page, slight exposure adaptation from the flashlight. Footsteps displace water; clothing darkens at the rain-soaked shoulders.

## Negative Prompt
No extra fingers, weapon deformation, floating paper, plastic skin, random camera shake, text, watermark, logo, or background figure duplication.

## Model Parameters
Seedance 2.5, 12 seconds, 16:9, live-action realism.

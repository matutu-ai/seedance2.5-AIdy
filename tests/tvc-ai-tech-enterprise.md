# TVC Test: Shenzhen AI Technology Enterprise

```text
Company: Shenzhen AI technology enterprise
Materials: company introduction, AI product brief, target enterprise customers
Objective: generate a 30-second brand/product TVC
Required: complete script, 12 shots, Seedance 2.5 prompt, voice-over, music direction
```

- Brand Insight, Big Idea, Theme, Story Concept, and 30-second script are present.

- Final prompt contains Commercial TVC, 30s, 16:9, character/product locks, camera/lens/movement, lighting/color/texture, voice-over/music, negative prompt, and end state.
- Quality report checks character, product, scene, commercial purpose, camera logic, and AI artifacts.

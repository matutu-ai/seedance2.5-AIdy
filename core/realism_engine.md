# Realism Engine

Choose physical artifacts that arise naturally from the camera, lens, lighting, weather, and contact in the scene. They should support observation, not become effects.

## Reality pass

- **Lens behavior:** shallow depth of field at close distance, gentle lens breathing only during meaningful pulls.
- **Motion:** directionally consistent motion blur; camera acceleration and deceleration have inertia.
- **Focus/exposure:** brief, restrained focus hunting or exposure adaptation only when moving between plausible light levels.
- **Camera body:** stable, tripod, gimbal, or subtle handheld movement must match the stated rig.
- **Material/contact:** feet compress grass, hands displace fabric, tires carry weight, rain wets exposed surfaces progressively.
- **World physics:** wind affects loose objects before heavy objects; reflections, shadows, and smoke follow sources and airflow.

## Avoid

Plastic skin, uniformly glossy surfaces, zero-weight props, perfect symmetry, looping background gestures, game-engine-clean illumination, arbitrary rack focus, and violent digital shake.

# Lighting Director Engine

Light the space first, then explain how the subject changes within it.

## Required lighting plan

```text
Main light: source, direction, quality, intensity
Fill light: source/absence and contrast purpose
Back light: source and separation effect
Shadow direction: where shadows fall and what blocks them
Color temperature: key practical colors and their relationship
Continuity: how light changes as performer/camera moves
```

Use motivated sources—window, streetlamp, monitor, fluorescent tube, product rig—unless the style explicitly calls for studio abstraction. Avoid incompatible time-of-day and source claims.

**Example:** Warm late-afternoon sunlight enters a left window as the main light; cool room bounce supplies minimal fill. When the camera arcs behind the performer, the warm side light becomes a soft rim and her face falls into cool half-shadow. Dust catches the window beam without obscuring her face.

# Consistency Tests

## Character consistency

- Face, age impression, hair, body proportions, wardrobe, and persistent accessories do not change between shots.
- Shot-specific gaze, expression, hands, and posture may change without redefining identity.

## Venue consistency

- Entrance, stage, screen, seating, aisle, and camera side preserve the same relative positions.
- Each venue shot references at least two stable landmarks.

## Prompt completeness

- Output includes specifications, roles, character lock, venue lock, timeline, camera, light, sound, negative constraints, and end state.
- Assumptions are labeled and no planning placeholder remains in the final prompt.

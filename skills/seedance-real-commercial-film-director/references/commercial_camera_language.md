# Commercial Camera Language

Choose one dominant movement per shot and motivate it:

| Movement | Commercial use | Constraint |
|---|---|---|
| Dolly in / push in | pressure, discovery, product confidence | define start/end framing and speed |
| Arc move | reveal relationship or venue geometry | define radius and screen direction |
| Tracking | match a person or product in motion | preserve landmark geography |
| Focus pull | transfer attention between subject planes | name both focus targets and timing |
| Crane | scale and final reveal | use only when vertical space is visible |

Specify height, distance, lens character, speed, inertia, focus, and light continuity. Avoid “cinematic shot” or compounded movements that cannot fit the duration.

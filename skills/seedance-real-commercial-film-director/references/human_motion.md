# Human Motion Reference

Direct causality rather than labels:

```text
attention → eyes → weight transfer → torso/limbs → head → hair/clothing → settle
```

Examples:

- Turn: eyes find the target, torso follows, head completes the turn, hair reacts with delayed inertia.
- Stand: feet anchor, weight moves forward, legs extend, pelvis and spine stack, clothing settles.
- Reach: gaze fixes, shoulder initiates, elbow extends, fingers close after contact, object carries weight.

Avoid synchronized body parts, zero-weight props, robotic blinking, floating feet, or repeated loops. Load only the modules visible at the selected shot distance.

# Quality Scorecard

Use this concise report after `prompt_checker.md`.

```markdown
Character Acting: __/25 — [one gap or strength]
Camera: __/25 — [one gap or strength]
Cinema: __/25 — [one gap or strength]
Reality: __/25 — [one gap or strength]
Total: __/100
Revision made: [only if under 80]
```

Quality is not the number of modifiers. A strong score has a legible dramatic progression, stable geography, motivated shot/light changes, and physical consequences.
